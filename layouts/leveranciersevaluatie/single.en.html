UC2: Supplier evaluation

Qualify your suppliers on data. Not on memory.

In most construction companies, supplier knowledge lives in one experienced buyer's head. When that person leaves, it goes with them. Dundir builds a structured, searchable supplier database from your own procurement history.

The problem

Certifications expire. A KOMO certificate is valid for three years, an SKG-IKOB assessment for two. In a normal procurement process, nobody tracks this systematically. The buyer remembers, until that buyer goes on holiday, falls ill, or leaves.

On large infrastructure projects, fewer than 5% of staff experience the project from start to finish. Institutional knowledge about supplier performance disappears systematically. Every new tender starts from zero.

How Dundir handles supplier evaluation

  • Certification management: The system registers KOMO, SKG-IKOB, Kiwa, and other relevant certifications per supplier, including expiry dates and alert moments before they lapse.
  • Performance history: Every completed assignment adds data: was the delivery on time? Did the specification match? What deviations were documented?
  • Structured scoring: Suppliers are scored on price, reliability, certification status, and sustainability performance. Scores are transparent and exportable.
  • Pre-qualification: For a new tender, the buyer immediately sees which suppliers meet the minimum requirements, without manually requesting certifications.

What it delivers

A structured supplier database that grows with every procurement decision. No more knowledge that walks out the door when a person leaves. Certification status always current. And a pre-qualification shortlist that accelerates the starting point of every tender.
