What Dundir does

Dundir is a locally deployed procurement decision system. For every procurement file it calculates the mathematically optimal supplier combination based on price, delivery time and quality criteria. The system automatically generates an audit-ready report. Your data never leaves your server. The procurement officer reviews and decides. Dundir handles the calculation and the documentation.

Who it's for

Mid-size contractors

50 to 500 employees. Procurement admin costs time that could be better spent. ROI is directly measurable in recovered hours. CPR 2024 sets new requirements for procurement documentation. Dundir delivers that documentation automatically.

Dutch municipalities

Every procurement decision must be demonstrably defensible. The Aanbestedingswet requires documented evaluation of supplier selection. There are 342 municipalities with virtually identical procurement processes. Dundir produces the required audit trail with every decision.
