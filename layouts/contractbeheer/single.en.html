The contract only matters when the materials pass the gate.

Dundir is developing the link between contract commitments and physical verification at the jobsite gate: mobile edge OCR, automated 3-way matching, and a Wkb Handover Dossier that builds up as deliveries arrive. Today this is a lab prototype on the bench; we are looking for a design partner to test it at a real gate.

The Reality at the Jobsite Gate

A contractor signs a competitive contract with supplier X: certified reinforcing steel, KOMO-certified facade elements, and delivery scheduled precisely at 07:00 AM. Three months later the truck arrives: delivery is two weeks delayed, the KOMO certificate is missing from the paper ticket, and delivered steel grade diverges from the purchase order.

Today, field verification relies on handwritten scribbles on paper clipboards and unorganized photos in WhatsApp groups. The empirical consequences:

Jobsite Gate Bottlenecks

• 70% of material delivery trucks wait up to 50 minutes at the site gate due to manual checking (Aldeeb 2024).
• 95% of paper delivery tickets contain discrepancies, incomplete records, or missing certificates (Qflow 2025).
• 8% to 25% of scheduled jobsite tasks are halted by material and delivery failures (Tserng et al. 2006).
• 4 to 6 week handover blockades under the Dutch Quality Assurance Act (Wkb) when inspection dossiers have to be reconstructed retroactively (VNG 2024).

The design: four functions of the Smart Gate

1. Edge OCR & License Plate Logging
A smartphone or fixed gate camera scans paper delivery tickets and license plates on arrival. Recognition is designed to run locally on edge hardware, so the gate keeps working without reliable site internet.

2. Automated 3-Way Matching
The system instantly reconciles original purchase orders (PO), scanned delivery orders (DO), and incoming vendor invoices. Quantity, dimension, or specification discrepancies are flagged before unloading commences.

3. Physical Chain of Custody & ADR
Immediate validation of environmental product declarations (EPD), KOMO quality marks, and ADR transport compliance for hazardous materials. Each delivery receives an immutable digital timestamp, weight record, and inspector ID.

4. Wkb Handover Dossier
Photos of inspection points, quality stamps, and delivery tickets are archived directly into the mandatory Dossier Bevoegd Gezag under the Wkb regulation. No missing documentation during final municipal commissioning.

Why This Is a Legal Mandate

For a general contractor, gate verification halts failure costs and idle trades. For public buyers and municipalities, it is a strict legal obligation.

Under the Dutch Quality Assurance Act (Wkb), a building cannot receive an occupancy permit without a certified Dossier Bevoegd Gezag. If delivered construction products fail to demonstrably satisfy building codes, the quality controller halts commissioning.

Under CPR 2024 (EU Construction Products Regulation 2024/3110), Digital Product Passports (DPP) and environmental declarations are legally required through the entire chain of custody. And under Public Procurement Act Article 2.130, public entities must prove that delivered reality matches the original award criteria.

Closing the Procurement Loop

The four modules operate as an integrated feedback system:

Procurement Package (UC3) structures requirements → Bid Comparison (UC1) computes the optimal allocation → Smart Gate (UC4) inspects physical deliveries and compiles the Wkb file → Supplier Evaluation (UC2) updates vendor performance metrics.

Every gate discrepancy is meant to recalibrate the scoring parameters for the next tender, so commercial intelligence stays within your organization when people leave.
