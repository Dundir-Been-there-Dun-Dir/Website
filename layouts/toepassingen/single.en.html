Four procurement phases. Backed by research.

Researchers at Aalto University, TU Delft, and KTH independently identified the same four phases as the highest-value opportunities for AI in construction procurement. Dundir covers all four.

UC1 — Bid comparison

Compare supplier bids in minutes, not hours. Deterministic. Reproducible. Full reasoning trail and audit-ready output for every recommendation.

UC2 — Supplier evaluation

Build and maintain a qualified supplier database, scored on price, reliability, certifications, and sustainability performance.

Read more

UC3 — Procurement package formation

From a plain-language specification to structured procurement requirements: hard requirements, weighted objectives, and a certification checklist ready for comparison.

Read more

UC4 — Contract oversight

Track whether suppliers deliver what they promised. Certification expiry alerts. Delivery deviation tracking. Audit trail of execution — not just selection.

Read more

Research basis

Joni Hamppi (Aalto University, 2025) identified four procurement phases as the highest-value AI opportunities in construction, based on interviews with procurement professionals at Skanska Talonrakennus Oy: bid comparison, supplier evaluation, procurement package formation, and contract oversight. The same phases are confirmed by research from TU Delft (Van Duuren, 2025) and Robert Gordon University (Adebayo, 2025).
