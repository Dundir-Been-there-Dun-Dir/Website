From Tender Award to Jobsite Gate and Handover Dossier.

Four modules for the full lifecycle of construction procurement and jobsite logistics, designed around a deterministic solver, mobile edge OCR, and an accumulated contract memory. In development with design partners: the solver in research and design, the smart gate as a lab prototype.

UC1: Bid Comparison & Best-Value Solver

Evaluate supplier bids mathematically and deterministically. Route-aware logistics, CPR 2024 compliance, and complete protection against generative AI numerical drift.

Read more

UC2: Supplier Evaluation & Knowledge Retention

Preserve vendor intelligence when personnel departs. Structured multi-criteria scoring across price, reliability, active certifications, and carbon intensity.

Read more

UC3: Procurement Package & Specification Structuring

Translate unstructured project specifications into quantified constraints, delivery windows, budget limits, and CPR 2024 compliance criteria.

Read more

UC4: Smart Gate & Jobsite Inspection / Wkb

Lab prototype: physical gate checks with edge OCR, 3-way matching across purchase orders, delivery receipts, and invoices, and real-time compilation of the Wkb Handover Dossier.

Read more

Research Foundation

Researchers at Aalto University (Hamppi 2025), TU Delft (Van Duuren 2025, Rockx 2023), and Robert Gordon University (Adebayo 2025) independently identified the same core stages as the highest-value opportunities for AI and optimization in construction: bid comparison, supplier evaluation, specification structuring, and physical gate verification. Dundir connects these four modules into a single closed feedback loop.
