Where the research points

Hamppi tested a generative AI tool on real bids at Skanska and found its numbers changed between sessions. At TU Delft, Rockx describes how supplier knowledge leaves large Dutch projects with their staff, and Van Duuren how generative AI adds checking work to infrastructure tenders. The literature supports the problem and the local design; whether Dundir's solver buys better than an experienced buyer is what the pilot has to show.
