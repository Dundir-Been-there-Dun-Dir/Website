The consequence

The word is not the only place it lands.

Measured per pairing, not per component

The accent has four jobs on this site: the word in the headline, the primary button, the material block in the cost stack, and small text on a white band. Brightening an orange helps the first and hurts the other three, because the same colour is being asked to sit on a very dark ground and on white.

The headline column is the accent itself, the small text column its light step. Below 4.5:1 a pairing cannot carry small text; below 3:1 it cannot carry display type either

The one that decides it Oranje at 3.01:1 on white means the primary button stops working as it is drawn today: white on that fill is unreadable, so the button would take dark text, and every accent word on a chalk band would have to step darker. That is a real design change, not a colour swap. Deep orange is a colour swap.
