Werktekening

A proposal for the next look of dundir.com. Construction runs on drawings: a line means a limit, a number means a measurement, and a stamp says who checked it. Werktekening borrows that grammar so the site reads like the work it describes. Everything above this sheet is a sample; everything below explains how it is built.

assets/css/werktekening.css, one file, no shared bundle
  • The drawing is the hero.

    The most characteristic thing in this trade is a constraint drawn on a plan. Pages open with a drawing that does the work: limits, the feasible area, the chosen point.

  • One brick point.

    Brick marks the optimum and the action, nothing else. If two unrelated things on a screen are brick, one of them is wrong.

  • Quiet type, exact figures.

    One family in two cuts. Geist sets everything people read; Geist Mono prints what a drawing would stamp: measurements, limits and the trace. Weight does the work, never size alone.

Colour

Warm paper is the page, the way a drawing is printed on a sheet. Graphite is the pencil and the dark band, used where the system shows itself. Brick is the only colour, fired clay rather than signal orange: the material most Dutch buildings are actually made of.

Type

One family, self-hosted: Geist for headlines and text, Geist Mono for figures. Headlines are semibold with tight tracking and stay at reading size rather than poster size; hierarchy comes from weight and tone. The second line of a headline steps down to the soft tone instead of growing.

Line

Three weights, borrowed from drawing practice: hair for grids and references, stroke for constraints and outlines, heavy for what you must not miss. Lines carry meaning, so a line that carries nothing is removed.

--hair 1px, --stroke 1.5px, --heavy 3px

Components

Few parts, square corners, no shadows. Each part answers one question a buyer has: what is it, how sure are you, where does that come from, what do I do next.

Motion

  • One moment per page: the plan draws itself on load, in the order a solver works. Limits, then the feasible area, then the cost line sweeping to the optimum.
  • Everything else moves only when someone acts: a tab, a slider, a button.
  • One curve, --ease, and three durations: fast, step, draw.
  • With reduced motion the plan appears drawn, and nothing animates.
