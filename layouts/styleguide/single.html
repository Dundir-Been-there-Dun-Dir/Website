Design system

The page is a decision under constraint.

Every token, band and component on dundir.com, rendered with the system itself. The written rationale lives in DESIGN.md; this page is the working reference. If something here looks wrong, the site is wrong.

{{/* 01 PRINCIPLE */}}

One idea, held everywhere

An optimizer does not offer an opinion. It removes everything that violates a constraint and returns what stands at the optimum. The site is built the same way: bands cut by hairlines, a ledger rail that stamps every claim, and exactly one accent colour that marks the chosen thing and nothing else.

Three rules carry most of the design:

  • The accent marks the optimum. One accented element per view: the primary action, the current page, the winning number. If everything is accented, nothing is.
  • One ground, two tones. The page is a single warm paper surface with a tint band for separation. Dark is spent only on the hero and the closing call, where the argument starts and ends. Hairlines and space do the rest, so the page reads as one document rather than a stack of inverted blocks.
  • No claim without a source line. Every number has a citation set in mono underneath it.
{{/* 02 COLOUR */}}

Colour

Warm neutral grounds, one accent, and a cool data colour that lives only inside the optimizer figure. The grounds are deliberately not blue shifted: on a blue black band every cool element reads as a shade of the ground rather than as a category of its own, which is the mistake this palette corrects.

Amber is the accent. It was trialled against audit teal #0F6E63 and signal orange #C8481B, and it holds the mark and the hero better than either. The cost of that choice is a hard rule: at 1.39:1 on paper, amber can never be text there. It is a fill, a rule, a dot or a vertex, and nothing else.

Display numbers on paper are set in ink, not in the olive step: at 2.4rem the olive reads muddy next to black serif. Amber returns at full strength the moment there is a fill behind it, which is why the primary button, the proof block rule, the chip dots and the mark's vertex all carry it. On the dark surfaces the constraint lifts entirely and amber is text: 11.1:1 on the header, 10.6:1 on the band.

{{/* 04 BANDS */}}

Bands and the ledger rail

Sections are full width bands, separated by a hairline or by a change of ground. Nothing floats: no cards, no shadows, no rounded panels on grey.

The rail on the left of this band is not markup. Every band lays its container out as a two column grid, so a CSS counter and the band's data-stamp attribute produce the stamp you see rotated in the margin. Below 1080px the stamp folds inline above the heading. That is the site keeping its own audit trail, which is what the product sells.

{{/* 06 MARK */}}

The mark

{{ partial "mark.html" . }}

A feasible region with the optimum sitting on one of its vertices. The same idea as the hero figure, reduced to 22 pixels. It appears in the header, in the favicon, and on every slide of every deck. It is never redrawn in another colour: the region is blueprint, the vertex is hivis.

{{/* 07 MOTION AND ACCESS */}}

Motion and access

  • The hero optimizer is the only continuous motion on the site. 30fps, paused off screen and on a hidden tab, halved population under 760px.
  • Sections rise 8px and fade in once, staggered by 60ms. No parallax, no scroll-jacking, no carousel.
  • prefers-reduced-motion disables all of it and draws the optimizer's final converged frame instead, with the region, the optimum and the labels in place.
  • Focus rings use the accent at 3px with 3px offset, visible on both grounds. A skip link precedes the header.
  • Every text pairing meets AA in its own band. Metadata switches token between --steel and --steel-ink for exactly this reason.
{{/* 08 RULES */}}

What this design refuses

Never

  • Stock photography of handshakes, hard hats or glass towers
  • Blue gradient heroes and rounded card grids on grey
  • A second accent colour competing with the first
  • Numbers without a source line
  • Em-dashes, in any file. CI fails the build on them
  • The words on the forbidden list in .github/workflows/deploy.yml

Always

  • One h1 per page, and it is the page subject
  • A stamp on every band, in the language of the page
  • Dark reserved for the hero and the closing call
  • Mono for anything measured
  • Self-hosted fonts, no third party requests
  • A single CSS file and one 6 kB script, no framework
