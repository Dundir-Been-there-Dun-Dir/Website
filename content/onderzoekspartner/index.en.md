- eyebrow: The open question
  heading: Nobody has ever defined <em class="cut" style="font-style:normal">optimal</em>.
  body: 'The optimisation theory is not new. What is missing is the objective function: what exactly the optimal supplier means, weighed across price, delivery time, certification compliance, structural material properties and sustainability, inside Dutch construction procurement.


    That question cannot be answered with theory. It needs buyers who describe how the trade-off actually goes, which requirements are hard and which are negotiable, and where instinct beats arithmetic. That is what a research partner brings.


    The second open question is just as practical: at what data volume does this approach beat an experienced buyer. That too can only be answered with real procurement files.'
  source: Öksüz et al. 2025 states explicitly that research and tooling for construction procurement operations remains limited. Van Duuren 2025, TU Delft, names a standardised review tool as an open research direction.
