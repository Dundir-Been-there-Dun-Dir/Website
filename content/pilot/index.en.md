- eyebrow: Why a design partner
  heading: A generic model does not know your jobsite.
  body: 'The true operational strength of this platform is not an abstract algorithm. It is the real-world calibration: knowing which certifications are non-negotiable for you, which supplier delivers reliably despite a higher unit price, and which deviation from the specification is acceptable in practice. That knowledge exists in no general textbook; it resides in your procurement history and gate logs.

    At this stage we work with a select group of design partners rather than mass-market buyers. Whoever joins now joins while the architecture can still be tailored directly to your operational workflows.'
- tone: chalk
  eyebrow: The 10-week protocol
  heading: Four phases and a first result within 7 days.
  steps:
  - h: 'Week 1: Intake and closed tenders'
    p: 'You provide three to five closed tenders with the quotations received and your award criteria. Everything stays local inside your network and falls under an NDA.'
  - h: 'Within 7 days: your own tenders, recalculated'
    p: 'Within seven working days you see your closed tenders back: quotations structured, compared on your criteria, with the full reasoning trail next to the choice you made at the time. No hardware and no ERP integration needed.'
  - h: 'Weeks 3 to 8: Running alongside live tenders'
    p: 'Dundir calculates alongside your live tenders, next to your standard workflow. We calibrate together on what your buyers and estimators weigh most. Every difference between the advice and your choice is logged and discussed. The smart gate can be tested as an optional phase on one site once the prototype is ready for it.'
  - h: 'Weeks 9 and 10: Evaluation and decision'
    p: 'Joint evaluation of time recovered, quality of the advice, and the logged differences. Then you decide on a licence with yearly maintenance.'
